## Introduction

"Open Source Planet" is not a myth, but rather the largest planet in the galaxy with many unexplored and mysterious areas, brimming with the energy of "sharing" and "collaboration." Each resident of the seven planets possesses different talents or skills. As long as they complete the beginner's mission in the Open StarTer Village, they can permanently reside there and explore a wider world.

---

## Goal of the Game

Players win influence points by initiating different projects or helping other players carry out their projects. When the game ends, the player with the most influence points wins.

## Rules For Simplified Mode ⭐ (MVP Target)

### Set-up

1. Open the project board and the score & action board. Turn the score & action board to the **"B" side**.
2. Each player chooses a color, takes **all action point tokens** (4) and **all contribution tokens** (12) in that color, and places their score marker on the starting point of the score track.
3. Use only the **entry-level project cards** (1 and 2 stars). Shuffle the cards, give **2 cards to each player**, create a pile of remaining cards, and place the pile on the table. Advanced project cards are not used.
4. Shuffle the labor cards, draw and turn over **8 cards**, and place them in the labor card section. Create a pile of the remaining cards and place them next to the labor card pile section.
5. Find the event card **"The End of the World!"** and place it face down at the bottom of the event card pile. Use only event cards labeled **"B"**. Take **5 of them** (suggested; minimum 3, maximum 6), shuffle them, and place them on top of "The End of the World!" card. This is now the event card pile.
6. The player who stayed up the latest last night takes the **"starting player card"**.
7. Open Source Tree is **not used** in Simplified Mode. (There is no Open Source Tree on the B-side of the action board.)

**Key Simplified Mode Numbers:**
- Action points per turn: **4**
- Labor cards in section: **8**
- Event cards in pile: **5** (suggested; 2p=6, 3p=5, 4p=4 per FAQ)
- Project cards used: entry-level only (29 cards)
- Initiator contribution points per action: **4**
- Facilitator contribution points per action: **5**

### Game Flow

> **Round summary:**
> 1. Starting player flips 1 event card → affects all players this round
> 2. Each player takes their turn (clockwise)
> 3. Pass the "starting player card" clockwise → start a new round
> 4. If the event card is "The End of the World!", end the game after all players have taken their turns

#### 1. Event Phase

The player with the "starting player card" turns over 1 new event card and places it on top of the old event card. This is now the "event" for this round, and the event will affect all players.

#### 2. Action Phase

Each player starts their turn with **4 action point tokens** and may spend them on actions. **Players cannot repeat the same type of action in one turn.**

See the [game-board action transcription](./game-actions.md) for the printed
Side B symbols and a comparison with Standard Mode.

Available actions (a–f):

**a. Initiate a Project** — Cost: 2 action points
- Play 1 project card from your hand and place it on the project board.
- Place 1 contribution token in the **"project founder"** section of the card.
- Take 1 labor card from the labor card section that fits the project's demands. Put used labor cards in a "Labor Card Discard Pile" and fill up the labor card section.
- Place 1 contribution token at the 2nd square in the column of the talent matching your labor card at the contribution section on the project board.
- Gain **2 influence points** immediately; move score markers 2 steps.

**b. Recruit Talents** — Cost: 1 action point
- Take 1 labor card from the labor card section that fits the demands of the project on the board. (Required talents and contribution points are shown on the project cards.)
- Place 1 contribution token at the 2nd square in the column of the talent matching the labor card at the contribution section on the project board.
- Take 1 card from the labor card pile and place it in the labor card section.
- You cannot recruit talents whose contribution sections are already full.

**c. Talent Scouting** — Cost: 1 action point
- Remove any number of cards from the labor card section and fill it up right away.
- Gain **1 influence point** immediately.

**d. Initiator Contribution** — Cost: 1 action point
- Move your token forward in the contribution section of **your own** projects.
- Gain **4 contribution points**.
- You can split the points across one project or talent column, or allocate them however you want across projects and talent columns, but you cannot exceed the demands of the projects.

**e. Facilitator Contribution** — Cost: 1 action point
- Move your token forward in the contribution section of **other players'** projects.
- Gain **5 contribution points**.
- You can split the points however you want, but cannot exceed project demands.

**f. Doin' Overtime** — Cost: 1 action point
- Repeat one **one-action-point** action you have already completed this turn.
- (Only actions that cost 1 action point can be repeated.)

> ★ The required talents and the required number of contribution points of those talents are shown on the project cards.

#### C. Finish a Project (if no project is finished, skip this step)

**When is a project finished?**
When the required number of contribution points for all required talent columns of a project is completed.

**Settlement:**
1. Each player who contributed gets **1 influence point** for every contribution point they contributed to the project.
2. The **initiator** gains an additional **2 influence points**.
3. The **player who finished the project** gains an additional **2 influence points**.
   - *(The initiator is also allowed to be the one who finished the project — they receive both bonuses.)*
4. Players take back their contribution tokens.

#### D. Restore Setting

- Players retrieve all action point tokens.
- Draw new project cards up to the maximum number of cards in hand.
- *(In Simplified Mode, no advanced card draw option.)*

#### 3. Ending Phase

If the event card for this round is **"The End of the World!"**, end the game after every player has taken their turns. If not, start a new round. Pass the "Starting player card" to the player on your left.

### Who Is The Winner?

Calculate contribution points of **unfinished projects**:
- Every **2 contribution points** on an unfinished project = **1 influence point**.
- Contribution points fewer than 2 are discarded.

Calculate the total influence points of each player from all projects. The player with the **most points wins**.

## Rules For Standard Mode

### Set-up

1. Open the project board and the score & action board. Make sure the score & action board is facing the **"A" side** up.
2. Each player chooses a color, takes **4 action point tokens** and **12 contribution tokens**, and places their score marker on the starting point of the score track and **1 action point token in the placing section for the 4th action point token** on the Open Source Tree.
3. Create a pile of **entry-level project cards** (1 and 2 stars) and a pile of **advanced-level project cards** (3 and 4 stars). After shuffling the entry-level cards, give **2 cards to each player** and place them next to the advanced-level project card pile.
4. Shuffle the labor cards, draw and turn over **6 cards**, and place them in the labor card section. Create a pile of the remaining cards.
5. Find the event card **"The End of the World!"** and place it face down at the bottom of the event card pile. Take event cards by player count — **2 players: 6 cards, 3 players: 5 cards, 4 players: 4 cards** — shuffle them, and place on top of "The End of the World!" This is the event card pile.
6. The player who stayed up the latest last night takes the **"starting player card"**.

**Key Standard Mode Numbers:**
- Action points per turn: **3** (upgradeable via Open Source Tree)
- Labor cards in section: **6**
- Event cards in pile: player-count dependent (2p=6, 3p=5, 4p=4)
- All 14 event cards used (including non-B cards)
- Initiator contribution points per action: **3**
- Facilitator contribution points per action: **4**

### Game Flow

Same overall structure as Simplified Mode (Event → Action → Finish Project → Restore), with these differences:

#### Action Phase Differences

See the [game-board action transcription](./game-actions.md) for the printed
Side A symbols, decoded effects, and a comparison with Simplified Mode.

**a. Initiate a Project** — Cost: 2 action points
- Same as Simplified, but initiator places contribution token at the **1st square** (not 2nd).
- Upon initiating, you must take 1 labor card from the labor card section and place it in the labor card section.
- Place 1 contribution token at the 1st square in the column of the talent matching your labor card at the contribution section on the project board.

**b. Recruit Talents** — Cost: 1 action point
- Place contribution token at the **1st square** (not 2nd) in the talent column at the contribution section.

**d. Initiator Contribution** — Cost: 1 action point
- Gain **3 contribution points** (vs. 4 in Simplified).

**e. Facilitator Contribution** — Cost: 1 action point
- Gain **4 contribution points** (vs. 5 in Simplified).

**d. Grow the Open Source Tree** — Cost: 1 action point *(Standard Mode only)*

See the [Open Source Tree board transcription](./open-source-tree.md) for every
branch, prerequisite, connection, reward, and collective ability.

- Place an Open Source Tree token in the corresponding heart-shaped section.
- Gain the number of influence points and activate collective ability immediately.
- The first two levels of the tree do not require any specific number of project cards; the remaining three levels require a certain amount and types of projects before they can be reached.
- You can only go upwards and to connected segments of the tree.
- Each level of the tree can only have a maximum of **two Open Source Tree tokens**.

#### Restore Setting (Standard Mode)

- Players retrieve all action point tokens and draw new project cards up to the maximum number in hand.
- If a player's influence points are **over 20**, they can choose to draw from the **advanced-level project pile**.

#### Collective Performance (Standard Mode only)

The number of remaining Open Source Tree tokens (not yet placed on the tree) reflects how well Open Starter Village grows:

| Remaining Tokens | Outcome |
|-----------------|---------|
| 0 | Our efforts led to the trends of open source, open data, and open government. The Open Source Tree has flourished! |
| 1–3 | Little aliens learned plenty of open-source concepts and are trying to open up all kinds of knowledge. The tree was glowing! |
| 4–6 | Some aliens grasped the spirit of openness. The tree is gradually recovering from its dormant period. |
| 7+ | The little aliens forgot their original intention. The Open Source Tree began to wither! |

## Scoring Reference

### Settlement When a Project is Completed

| Bonus | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| Per contribution point contributed | +1 VP each |
| Initiator bonus | +2 VP |
| Finisher bonus (player who completes the project) | +2 VP |

*(Initiator and finisher can be the same player — both bonuses apply.)*

### End-Game Scoring for Unfinished Projects

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Every 2 contribution points on unfinished project | +1 VP |
| Fewer than 2 contribution points | discarded |

## Upgraded Action and Suspended Action

- **Upgraded action tile**: When growing the Open Source Tree upgrades an action, cover the action tile with the Upgraded action tile to remind players.
- **Suspended action tile**: Some event cards suspend an action for the round (e.g., "GitHub is Down"). Cover that action tile with the Suspended action tile as a reminder.

### Advanced-Level Project Cards (Standard Mode Only)

When "Restore Setting" occurs, if a player's influence points are **over 20**, they can choose to draw a card from the advanced-level project pile.

## FAQ

**Q1: When recruiting talents, if contribution tokens have been placed in a talent column, can players still select a labor card of that talent?**

A1: You cannot select a labor card of that talent if you already have your token on the corresponding talent column. If only other players' tokens are in that column, you can still select a labor card of that talent, but the amount of contribution squares you contribute should not exceed the required amount of the project.

**Q2: What if contribution tokens of different players are at the same square in the same talent column in the contribution section?**

A2: Stack them on the same square.

**Q3: Are there always 5 event cards in the simplified edition?**

A3: It's just the suggested number. The number of cards can be adjusted based on game length, but 6 is the maximum. If time is insufficient, it is suggested to have at least 3 event cards for a thorough experience. (The card "The End of the World!" is excluded.)

**Q4: [Standard Mode] Will the collective abilities received from growing the Open Source Tree be effective immediately?**

A4: Yes, all collective abilities from growing the Open Source Tree will be effective immediately. For example, if a player reaches the third level of the tree and receives the "collective ability" of "gaining 4 action points each round instead of 3", even if the player who receives this effect has spent all their action points, they can still take the 4th action point they just received and use it.

**Q5: [Standard Mode] When a player reaches the fifth level of the Open Source Tree and it thus only takes one action point to initiate a project, can they use the "Doin' overtime" card to initiate another project?**

A5: Yes. Players can repeat any one-action-point action by playing the "Doin' overtime" card. Therefore, when it only takes 1 action point to initiate a project, players can choose to initiate another project — a player can initiate at most 2 projects in a round.

**Q6: What should players do if a project requires more than 12 contribution points, but there are not enough squares on the board?**

A6: Just put it on the next column and keep accumulating the contribution points.
