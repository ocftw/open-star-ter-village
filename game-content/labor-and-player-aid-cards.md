## Player-aid card

The player-aid content spans PDF pages 9 and 10.

### 1. Initiate a project (+2 points)

Spend two action points to play a project card in your hand. Take one labor
card from the labor card section that fits the demands of this project and
place one contribution token in the “project founder” section of he card and
the other in the corresponding contribution section.

### 2. Recruit Talents

Spend one action point to take one labor card from the labor card section that
fits thedemands of the project card on the board and place a contribution token
in the corresponding contribution section.

### 3. Talent Scouting (+1 point)

Spend one action point to remove any number of cards from the labor card
section and replenish right away.”

### 4. Growing the Open Source Tree (standard edition only)

Spend one action point to grow the Open Source Tree (place the Open Source Tree
token in the corresponding heart-shaped section). You can only go upwards and
to the connected segments of the tree. Each level of the tree can only have a
maximum of two Open Source Tree tokens.

### 5. Initiator Contribution

Spend one action point to move forward the token in the contribution section of
your own projects. You can split up your points however you want across your
projects.

### 6. Facilitator Contribution

Spend one action point to move forward the token in the contribution section of
other players' projects. You can split up your points however you want across
other players' projects that you contributed.

### 7. Doin' Overtime

Spend one action point to repeat one of the one-action-point tasks you have
already completed this turn.
