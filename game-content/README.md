## Transcription notes

- The first user-provided PDF is titled `（英）開源專案` in its metadata and
  contains project cards, not a game board. It is therefore represented as
  `project-cards.md`.
- The game-board PDF is titled `（英）星源樹` in its metadata. Its A-side
  contains the Open Source Tree; its B-side is the Simplified Mode action and
  labor-card board.
- Card names, descriptions, and effects follow the printed English material,
  including apparent spelling and wording errors. Formatting, whitespace, and
  typographic ligatures are normalized for Markdown.
- Chinese talent labels on project cards are mapped to the English labor-card
  names found in the job-card PDF.
- The project-card PDF contains 28 entry-level cards and 11 advanced cards,
  based on the printed star counts. This differs from the rulebook inventory,
  which says 29 entry-level and 10 advanced cards.
- The PDFs were parsed with text extraction and verified against rendered page
  images. Page numbers below refer to PDF page numbers.
