What the sweep shows

    Scenario heat map

    Rows: starter rotation × extra rounds. Columns: tree preset × completion reward. Each cell pools every bot seating in the run. Seat spread is the strongest seat's win rate minus the weakest seat's, each averaged over all games in the cell (ties split the win); S4 › S1 names the two seats. Seat deviation adds up every seat's distance from a fair share (1 ÷ players). pp means percentage points, the plain difference between two percentages: 31% − 14% = 17 pp. Hover a cell for its numbers.
